## Hobbies

### Riichi Mahjong

Riichi mahjong is a four-player tile game involving strategy, probability, and imperfect information. I play both online and in New York City. I placed 2nd in the [Intercollegiate Riichi Mahjong Open Finals](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QNxIwyyidiM) in 2025, which qualified me for a seeded entry in the [2026 Ryuousen USA Qualifiers](https://www.saikouisen-usa.com/ryuousen-usa-qualifiers).

I also enjoy exploring mahjong from a computational perspective for fun. Its combination of imperfect information and stochasticity makes it interesting from the perspective of AI and reinforcement learning. Recently, I have been playing with [Mahjax: A GPU-Accelerated Mahjong Simulator for Reinforcement Learning in JAX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20577).

I am also building a small website for mahjong analysis and tools. Check it out here:

[Riichi Mahjong Tools](https://riichi-mahjong.github.io/riichi-mahjong-tools/)
